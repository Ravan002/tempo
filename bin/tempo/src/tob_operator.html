How a round works

  1. The VRF picks the leader of view v from the certificate of view v−1. As soon as the leader starts proposing, it opens its lane and announces itself to the operator over the authenticated top-of-block channel.
  2. The operator immediately flushes the latest quote of every maker to that leader, then streams each new quote the moment it is signed, until the lane window closes.
  3. A newer quote from a maker replaces its older one at the leader, keeping the maker's slot. Validators that do not lead receive nothing.
  4. After its start-of-block system work, the builder executes the lane first, then fills from the pool. Public transactions pay a far higher tip () and still come after.
  5. The dashboard reads every block back from a different validator's RPC and checks that the lane transactions are the block's first transactions.
